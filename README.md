# AptoCAD Finance (working title)

A planned **Windows desktop app for company income and expense tracking** at AptoCAD Engineering. It
replaces the *AptoCAD Department Finance Tracker* Excel workbook with something faster, safer and nicer to use.

- Tracks the **Civil** and **Mechanical** departments as separate profit centres, plus **Corporate / Shared** costs
- Reports in **LKR** while keeping the original currency (USD, CAD, GBP, EUR) and FX rate
- Handles Upwork/Payoneer payouts, moves between accounts and department transfers without double counting
- Shows project profitability, payroll and dashboards you can click through to the underlying entries
